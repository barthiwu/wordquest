# WordQuest Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

WordQuest is a language-learning app. This policy explains what information WordQuest collects, why, and what choices you have about it. It's written in plain language rather than dense legal text — if anything here isn't clear, email us (contact info below) and we'll clarify or fix it.

WordQuest is currently developed and operated by an independent developer, not a registered company. For all privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact:

**bartholomewiwuoha@gmail.com**

## Who can use WordQuest

You must be **at least 13 years old** to create a WordQuest account. This is enforced at signup — we ask for your date of birth and reject registration if you're under 13. We do this to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), which restricts collecting personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. WordQuest does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete that account and the data associated with it. If you believe a child under 13 has created a WordQuest account, email us and we'll investigate and remove it.

## Information we collect

**Account information you provide directly:**
- Email address and password (your password is never stored in plain text — it's hashed before it touches our database)
- Display name
- Date of birth (used only to verify you meet the minimum age — see above)
- Country (optional, used to show your flag and for country/continent leaderboards)
- Native language and the language you're learning

**Information generated as you use the app:**
- Gameplay and learning progress: quiz answers, mastery scores, XP, streaks, achievements, Boss Battle results, and similar progression data
- Word in the Wild submissions: text or photos you submit as evidence of using a word in the real world. Photos are stored so they can be evaluated and so you can view your own submission history; if you delete a submission, the photo and text are removed from our storage (see "Your choices" below).
- Clan membership, if you join one
- Device timezone (used to figure out your "day" for streaks and daily quests — never your precise location)
- Push notification token, if you enable notifications
- Basic security/audit logs (login attempts, password resets) used to protect your account from unauthorized access

**Information we do not collect:** WordQuest does not access your contacts, precise GPS location, or other apps on your device. We don't buy or sell personal information, and we don't use your data for third-party advertising.

## How we use your information

- To run the app: authenticate you, save your progress, calculate mastery/XP/streaks, and show leaderboards
- To evaluate Word in the Wild submissions and sentence/paragraph writing exercises using AI (see "Third parties" below)
- To send account-related email (email verification, password reset) and, if you enable them, push notifications about your progress
- To keep the app secure (detecting suspicious login activity, rate-limiting abuse)
- To understand how the app is used in aggregate, so we can improve it (see "Analytics" below)

## Analytics

WordQuest logs basic usage events (e.g., account created, quest completed, Boss Battle played) to our own backend and database — not to a third-party analytics company. This data is used only to understand how the app is used in aggregate and improve it; it is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build advertising profiles.

## Third parties we work with

WordQuest relies on a small number of service providers to operate. Each only receives the specific data needed to do its job:

- **Anthropic (Claude API)** — evaluates Word in the Wild evidence (photos and text) and sentence/paragraph writing exercises, to check whether you've used a word correctly. Submitted text and photos are sent to Anthropic's API for this evaluation.
- **Resend** — sends transactional email (email verification, password reset). Your email address is shared with Resend only to deliver these messages.
- **Expo (push notifications)** — delivers push notifications to your device, if you enable them.
- **Cloudflare R2 / S3-compatible object storage** — stores Word in the Wild photo uploads.
- **Railway** — hosts our backend application and database.
- **Sentry** (only if enabled on a given environment) — receives error/crash reports to help us fix bugs. These reports do not include your password or full submission content.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), it's deactivated immediately and can be recovered for a period of time if you change your mind; after that, it's a soft-deleted record that no longer functions as a live account. If you want your data permanently and completely erased rather than soft-deleted, email us and we'll process that request.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access or export your data:** email us and we'll provide a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct your data:** most profile fields can be edited directly in the app (Settings); email us for anything you can't change yourself.
- **Delete your account or data:** use Delete Account in Settings, or email us to request full erasure.
- **Delete a Word in the Wild submission:** you can delete your own submissions from within the app; this removes the photo and text content.
- **California residents (CCPA):** you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of its "sale." WordQuest does not sell personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of on that front — but the access/deletion rights above apply the same way for California residents as for everyone else.
- **Push notifications:** you can disable these at any time in Settings or your device's notification settings.

## Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text). Sessions use short-lived access tokens with longer-lived refresh tokens, and we lock accounts temporarily after repeated failed login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard steps to protect your information.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where the change is significant, let you know in the app.
